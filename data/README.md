# Data Workspace

This folder is the traffic/public-finance data scaffold. It is for contracts, placeholders, and
templates only.
Do not commit real datasets, scraped files, credentials, API keys, or product-specific assumptions here.

## Folder Roles

- `raw/`: original SUMO networks, routes, or manually received files. Keep real files local or in Drive.
- `interim/`: temporary transformed simulation inputs. Keep real files local or in Drive.
- `processed/`: model-ready metrics generated by reproducible scripts. Keep real files local or in Drive.
- `external/`: public-finance assumptions or source extracts. Keep real files local or in Drive.
- `catalog/`: small metadata templates that are safe to commit.

## Week 1 Rule

The Economics/Public Finance team defines assumptions and policy scenarios. The CS team defines how
those assumptions become SUMO-RL inputs and reproducible outputs.

Use the catalog templates in this order:

1. `source_registry.template.csv`: list public-finance and traffic assumption sources.
2. `variable_dictionary.template.csv`: define traffic, cost, and policy variables.
3. `crawl_plan.template.csv`: convert approved sources into a technical collection or manual-entry plan.

No real collection starts until the team has reviewed source reliability, legal/crawl constraints,
update frequency, and expected output format.
